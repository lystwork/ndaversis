# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled by the **NDAVERSIS** software.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Data Collection**: User data is **not** used, downloaded, stored, or sent by the creator of this repository. The software operates locally on your machine.
- **Local Autonomy**: When used without AI functionality, all data processing stays strictly within your local environment. The creator of this repository has no access to your code, your data, or your usage patterns.

## 2. AI Services (Optional)
If you explicitly configure and use AI features (e.g., by providing API keys for Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or DeepSeek, or by connecting to on-premise AI/LLM/RAG solutions):
- **Third-Party Policies**: In cases where AI is used, the privacy policies and rules of the respective AI providers or your own on-premise infrastructure owners apply. 
- **Data Transmission**: Only the necessary code snippets and change summaries required for documentation generation are sent to your configured AI service. This data is not sent to, stored, or accessible by the creator of this repository.

## 3. Data Storage
The software saves its state in a local file (`ndaversis_state.json`) and writes internal logs to `ndaversis_logs.py`. This data exists solely on your computer.

## 4. Updates to This Policy
This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Any changes will be reflected in this document.

## 5. Contact
For questions regarding this policy, please contact us via the channels listed in the README.
